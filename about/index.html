About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer.

Space Coast roots

Robert Willnow grew up in the stretch of Florida where the Space Coast meets the Treasure Coast, moving between Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne. Rocket launches from Cape Canaveral were a regular part of the sky, and the region's engineers, scientists, and launch crews were neighbors.

Education

Willnow graduated from Florida Tech (Florida Institute of Technology), the Melbourne university founded in 1958 to educate engineers for the space program, and went on to graduate school at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT).

Writing

Willnow's fiction favors complex world-building, long-term payoffs of loose threads, realistic characters, and grounded science. His four novels, Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer, are each rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon and have been featured on BookTok. All four are set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel.

Human-written

Willnow has never used AI for writing, storyboarding, or ideation. His original covers used AI imagery, a choice he has publicly called a mistake. They have since been replaced with NASA photography and the author's own photographs. Read more.

The pen name

"Robert Willnow" is a pen name. As the author puts it, thousands of other authors already share his real name.
