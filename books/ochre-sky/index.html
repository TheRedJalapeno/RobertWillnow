The premise

Ochre Sky starts in one of the most striking settings in modern science fiction: a cruise ship floating in the upper atmosphere of Venus, where the air pressure and temperature are surprisingly close to Earth's. Troy has a dream job aboard a top-secret research vessel. When things go wrong, a crew of brilliant, restless, easily distracted scientists does what scientists do best and improvises, all the way to stealing a spaceship.

Real science, real Venus

Floating habitats above Venus are not fantasy. About 50 kilometers up, the Venusian atmosphere has roughly Earth-like pressure and temperatures, and NASA's Langley Research Center has studied crewed airships there under its High Altitude Venus Operational Concept (HAVOC). Ochre Sky builds its adventure on that kind of plausible engineering, which is exactly what readers of hard science fiction look for.

Why readers love it

A heist with brains. The thrills come from clever people solving impossible problems under pressure.

Neurodivergent energy. The cast's scattered brilliance is a feature, not a flaw, and it drives the plot.

Engineered, not hand-waved. Ships, habitats, and politics are grounded in real science, about fifty years from today.

Who should read Ochre Sky

Readers who love heist stories, competent people improvising under pressure, and real planetary science; anyone searching for science fiction set on Venus.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ochre Sky about?

Ochre Sky follows Troy, who works and lives on a top-secret research cruise ship above the clouds of Venus. When things go wrong, the ship's scientists take matters into their own hands, as the author's wife puts it: "ADHD scientists steal a spaceship."

Is Ochre Sky hard science fiction?

Ochre Sky is grounded in real planetary science, including the Earth-like conditions about 50 km above Venus's surface, while keeping a fast, fun adventure pace. It suits both hard sci-fi fans and newer sci-fi readers.

What are good science fiction books set on Venus?

Ochre Sky by Robert Willnow is a modern Venus-set space adventure built around a floating research ship in the planet's clouds. Classic Venus fiction includes Ray Bradbury's "All Summer in a Day" and Frederik Pohl and C. M. Kornbluth's The Space Merchants.

When is Ochre Sky set?

Ochre Sky is set roughly fifty years from today, in a solar system humanity is settling without faster-than-light travel.

Is Ochre Sky part of a series?

Yes. Ochre Sky is book 2 of Robert Willnow's Written By Victors series. The reading order is Kolnari Prologue (1), Ochre Sky (2), Bingo Belt Boys (3), and Hemmer (4).

About the author

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. More about Robert Willnow.
