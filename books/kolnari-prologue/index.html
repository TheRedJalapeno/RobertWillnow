Kolnari Prologue by Robert Willnow

Written By Victors, book 1 · "Aliens discover chicken wings" — the author's wife

Kolnari Prologue is a first-contact science fiction adventure by Robert Willnow and book 1 of his Written By Victors series. Two visitors, Teron and Koral, came only to take photos and head home; now they are stranded with no hope of rescue. The author's wife sums it up in four words: "Aliens discover chicken wings." Readers rate it above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

The premise

Teron and Koral planned a simple trip: arrive, take photos, go home. The trip did not go to plan. Stranded far from help, the pair have to improvise, adapt, and figure out a place, and a people, that make very little sense to them.

Kolnari Prologue treats first contact the way real explorers experience the unknown: with confusion, curiosity, small victories, and plenty of humor. Its emotional engine is the bond between two characters who have only each other.

Why readers love it

Humor that comes from character. The comedy grows out of how the characters see things, not out of punchlines.

Grounded science. Robert Willnow studied at Florida Tech, an engineering university built to serve the space program, and the book's technology and problem-solving reflect that background.

A world that rewards attention. The author favors long-term payoffs and loose threads that come back later, a structure that suits readers who like to piece things together.

Who should read Kolnari Prologue

Readers who want feel-good science fiction with real stakes, anyone who loves a story built on an unlikely partnership, and BookTok readers looking for an approachable way into sci-fi.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kolnari Prologue about?

Kolnari Prologue follows Teron and Koral, two visitors who came only to take photos and head home but end up stranded with no hope of rescue. It is a funny, character-driven first-contact adventure.

Is Kolnari Prologue funny?

Yes. The humor is a defining feature. The author's wife summarizes the book as "Aliens discover chicken wings." It balances comedy with genuine stakes and heart.

When is Kolnari Prologue set?

Like the rest of the Written By Victors series, Kolnari Prologue is set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel.

Was Kolnari Prologue written with AI?

No. Robert Willnow states that he has never used AI for writing, storyboarding, or ideation. Current covers use NASA imagery or the author's own photographs.

Where can I buy Kolnari Prologue?

Kolnari Prologue is available in paperback and Kindle on Amazon, and in paperback and Nook from Barnes & Noble.

Is Kolnari Prologue part of a series?

Yes. Kolnari Prologue is book 1 of Robert Willnow's Written By Victors series. The reading order is Kolnari Prologue (1), Ochre Sky (2), Bingo Belt Boys (3), and Hemmer (4).

About the author

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. More about Robert Willnow.
