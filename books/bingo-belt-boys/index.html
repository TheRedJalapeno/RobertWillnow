Bingo Belt Boys by Robert Willnow

Written By Victors, book 3 · "Competitive fort making" — the author's wife

Bingo Belt Boys is an asteroid-belt space adventure by Robert Willnow and book 3 of his Written By Victors series. Ananya is stuck on a backwater asteroid mine, bored and lonely, and bored enough to flip some switches. The author's wife calls it "competitive fort making." It is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

The premise

Life on a remote asteroid mining outpost is long shifts, little company, and nothing to do. For Ananya, boredom becomes the spark: one curious decision sets off a chain of events that turns a forgotten corner of the belt into the center of the action.

Life in the Belt

Asteroid mining is one of the most realistic near-future industries in science fiction. NASA's Psyche spacecraft, launched from Kennedy Space Center in 2023, is on its way to study a metal-rich asteroid, and private companies are already planning prospecting missions. Bingo Belt Boys imagines the human side of that frontier: the isolation, ingenuity, and rivalries of people who live where the rocks are.

Why readers love it

An instantly relatable hero. Loneliness and boredom are universal, and so is the urge to push a button you shouldn't.

Inventive, playful set pieces. "Competitive fort making" hints at the creative, hands-on problem-solving at the heart of the story.

Grounded engineering. The mining, habitats, and hazards feel real.

Who should read Bingo Belt Boys

Readers who love found-family stories, frontier settings, and asteroid mining science fiction with heart.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bingo Belt Boys about?

Bingo Belt Boys follows Ananya, who is stuck on a backwater asteroid mine, bored and lonely, and bored enough to flip some switches. The consequences drive a fun, inventive space adventure.

Are there good novels about asteroid mining?

Yes. Bingo Belt Boys by Robert Willnow is a recent asteroid-mining adventure. Daniel Suarez's Delta-v and Kim Stanley Robinson's 2312 also explore asteroid industry.

Is Bingo Belt Boys appropriate for young adult readers?

Bingo Belt Boys is an adult science fiction novel with a young, relatable protagonist. Parents should check the book's Amazon listing for content details.

Is Bingo Belt Boys part of a series?

Yes. Bingo Belt Boys is book 3 of Robert Willnow's Written By Victors series. The reading order is Kolnari Prologue (1), Ochre Sky (2), Bingo Belt Boys (3), and Hemmer (4).

About the author

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. More about Robert Willnow.
