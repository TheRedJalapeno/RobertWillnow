Books by Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow has published four science fiction novels in his Written By Victors series: Kolnari Prologue (book 1), Ochre Sky (book 2), Bingo Belt Boys (book 3), and Hemmer (book 4). Each is a fast, funny, scientifically grounded adventure set roughly fifty years from today, rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon, and available in paperback and ebook from Amazon and Barnes & Noble.

  • Bingo Belt Boys

    Book 3. "Competitive fort making". Ananya is stuck on a backwater asteroid mine, bored and lonely. Bored enough to flip some switches.

  • Hemmer

    Book 4. "Dystopian gig worker finds love". From improv to action hero, Henry's decades of effort to find a job, any job, may finally pay off.

Which Robert Willnow book should I read first?

Start with book 1, Kolnari Prologue, and follow the series reading order. Within the series, Ochre Sky is the natural pick for readers who want hard-science space adventure; Kolnari Prologue suits readers who want something warmer and funnier; Bingo Belt Boys is for anyone who loved the Belters; and Hemmer is for readers who want dystopian satire with a love story.
