Hemmer by Robert Willnow

Written By Victors, book 4 · "Dystopian gig worker finds love" — the author's wife

Hemmer is a near-future dystopian science fiction novel by Robert Willnow and book 4 of his Written By Victors series. From improv to action hero, Henry's decades of effort to find a job, any job, may finally pay off. The author's wife's summary: "Dystopian gig worker finds love." The premise

Henry has spent decades hustling through a gig economy that never quite lets anyone get ahead. He has done improv, odd jobs, and everything in between. Then the strangest gig of all arrives, one that could turn a lifetime of scrambling into something heroic, and bring him love along the way.

Why Hemmer hits home

Hemmer takes an economy built on side hustles, app-based work, and constant reinvention, and pushes it a few steps further. The result is satire that is funny because it is familiar, and an action story with an underdog readers root for from page one.

Why readers love it

An underdog hero. Henry's persistence is both funny and moving.

Action plus romance. Hemmer delivers set pieces and a love story in the same book.

Sharp social science fiction about work, worth, and what people will do for a paycheck.

Who should read Hemmer

Readers who like dystopian fiction with a sense of humor, BookTok readers who want romance mixed into their sci-fi, and anyone who has ever juggled three gigs to pay rent.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hemmer about?

Hemmer follows Henry, a gig worker in a dystopian near future whose decades of effort to find a job, any job, from improv to action hero, may finally pay off, and who finds love along the way.

Is Hemmer a romance?

Hemmer is science fiction and action first, with a meaningful love story woven through it. The author's wife summarizes it as "Dystopian gig worker finds love."

Are there novels about the gig economy?

Yes. Hemmer by Robert Willnow is a near-future dystopian take on gig work. Related reads include Kurt Vonnegut's Player Piano and Boots Riley's film Sorry to Bother You.

Is Hemmer part of a series?

Yes. Hemmer is book 4 of Robert Willnow's Written By Victors series. The reading order is Kolnari Prologue (1), Ochre Sky (2), Bingo Belt Boys (3), and Hemmer (4).

About the author

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. More about Robert Willnow.
