Robert Willnow is a breakout science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast, a Florida Tech graduate who went on to graduate school at RIT. His Written By Victors series of fast, funny, scientifically grounded space adventures is set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel, and every title is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

Hello! I am Robert Willnow, a pen name because ten thousand Robert Allens have already published books. My preference is for complex world building, long-term payoffs of loose threads, and putting myself into each character.

AI: I do not and have never used AI in any capacity for my writing, storyboarding, or ideation. I DID use it for original covers. When publishing initially, I mistakenly believed my only options were professional cover artists (beyond my budget) or AI. This misrepresented my work, and I regret that choice. After NASA's Artemis mission, I realized NASA photos are free to use. Now my covers come from either NASA or my own camera roll, and they are far better than the AI slop. Sorry about that.

If you like fun adventures, realistic characters, classic science fiction, or grounded science, you will enjoy my work.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Robert Willnow?

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer.

When are Robert Willnow's books set?

The Written By Victors series is set roughly fifty years from today, as humanity spreads across the solar system before faster-than-light travel exists.

What order should I read Robert Willnow's books in?

Read the Written By Victors series in order: Kolnari Prologue (1), Ochre Sky (2), Bingo Belt Boys (3), and Hemmer (4).

Are Robert Willnow's books written with AI?

No. Robert Willnow has never used AI for writing, storyboarding, or ideation. Current covers use NASA imagery or the author's own photographs.
