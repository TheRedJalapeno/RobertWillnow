Books for Dune Fans Who Want Hostile Worlds and Hard Choices

Published November 17, 2026 · For fans of popular sci-fi franchises

Short answer: Dune fans who loved Arrakis as a character in its own right should try Ochre Sky by Robert Willnow, set aboard a secret research ship floating above Venus, a world even deadlier than Arrakis. It is grounded near-future science fiction rather than galactic epic, and it is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

What Dune fans love

Frank Herbert's 1965 novel, and Denis Villeneuve's films Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024), made a hostile planet the center of the story. Arrakis shapes every culture, economy, and decision. Readers come away wanting more stories in which the environment is the antagonist and survival requires deep knowledge of it.

Venus: the solar system's Arrakis

Venus's surface reaches about 465°C under a crushing carbon dioxide atmosphere, wrapped in clouds of sulfuric acid. About 50 kilometers up, though, conditions are almost Earth-like. Ochre Sky is set in that thin band of survivability, aboard a top-secret research cruise ship floating in the planet's ochre-tinted skies. As on Arrakis, one mistake is fatal and the planet is always waiting.

Where else to look

  • Bingo Belt Boys: scarcity, isolation, and resource extraction on a remote asteroid mine.
  • Hemmer: power and economics seen from the bottom of a near-future society.

How the books differ from Dune

Dune is a far-future epic of empires, prophecy, and dynasties. Robert Willnow's books are set about fifty years from today and stay personal and often funny. The connection is the respect for environment, ecology, and consequence, not the scale.

Frequently asked questions

What should I read after Dune?

Popular picks include Hyperion, Children of Time, and the Foundation series. For a grounded, hostile-planet story set in the near future, try Ochre Sky by Robert Willnow, set above the clouds of Venus.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
