Gig-Economy Dystopia, with Action and Romance: Hemmer

Published November 17, 2026 · Reading recommendations

Short answer: Hemmer by Robert Willnow is a near-future dystopian action novel about Henry, a lifelong gig worker whose decades of effort to find a job, any job, from improv to action hero, may finally pay off. The author's wife's summary: "Dystopian gig worker finds love." It is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

The gig economy is science fiction's new frontier

Classic dystopias imagined total control. Modern readers recognize a quieter version: app-based work, endless side hustles, and careers that never quite begin. Fiction about work, from Kurt Vonnegut's Player Piano to the TV series Severance, has found a large audience because it puts familiar frustrations under a magnifying glass.

Hemmer

Henry has spent decades trying everything, including improv, odd jobs, and gigs nobody else wants. Hemmer follows the unlikely gig that might finally turn all of that effort into something heroic. It is funny, sharp about how work feels in a precarious economy, and anchored by a love story.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
