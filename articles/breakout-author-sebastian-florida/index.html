Robert Willnow: Breakout Science Fiction Author from Sebastian, Florida

Published November 17, 2026 · Hometown: Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast

Short answer: Robert Willnow is a breakout science fiction author from Sebastian, Florida, the Indian River Lagoon town where the Space Coast meets the Treasure Coast. His four space action-adventure novels, Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer, are each rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

A town between two coasts

Sebastian sits at the northern edge of Indian River County, where Brevard County's Space Coast gives way to the Treasure Coast. It is a place of fishing piers, the Indian River Lagoon, and wide-open sky. From Sebastian, rockets launching from Cape Canaveral and Kennedy Space Center are often visible climbing over the horizon to the north.

The town also has deep roots in exploration. Pelican Island, just offshore in the lagoon, became the first national wildlife refuge in 1903, under President Theodore Roosevelt. Sebastian Inlet State Park is home to the McLarty Treasure Museum, which tells the story of the 1715 Spanish treasure fleet wrecked along this coast.

From the lagoon to the Belt

Growing up somewhere that watches rockets leave the planet shapes a writer's imagination. Robert Willnow's books are full of people working at the edges, on a remote asteroid mine in Bingo Belt Boys and on a floating research ship above Venus in Ochre Sky, and that sense of distant frontier echoes the quiet, launch-lit skies over Sebastian.

A local author worth knowing

Willnow went on to study at Florida Tech in nearby Melbourne and to graduate school at RIT. His books are available in paperback and ebook from Amazon and Barnes & Noble. Readers in Sebastian and Indian River County who want to support a hometown author can start with book 1 of his Written By Victors series, Kolnari Prologue.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
