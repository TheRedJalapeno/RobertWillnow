Books Like The Expanse: Why Readers Are Turning to Robert Willnow

Published November 17, 2026 · For fans of popular sci-fi franchises

Short answer: If you loved The Expanse by James S. A. Corey, Robert Willnow is one of the strongest new names to try. His four novels, Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer, combine believable technology, near-future solar-system settings, and fast action with more humor than most hard sci-fi. Together they form the Written By Victors series, and each is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon. Start with book 1, Kolnari Prologue.

What makes a book "like The Expanse"?

Across nine novels, from Leviathan Wakes (2011) to Leviathan Falls (2021), and the television adaptation, The Expanse built a loyal audience with a specific combination of qualities. Readers looking for a follow-up usually want most of these:

  • Plausible physics. Ships have mass, acceleration hurts, and the solar system is huge.
  • A lived-in solar system of Belters, stations, mining outposts, and the politics between them.
  • Working-class heroes, ordinary people pulled into extraordinary events.
  • Momentum. Chapters end on hooks, and the action never stalls for long.
  • Payoffs. Threads planted early come back in satisfying ways.

Robert Willnow's novels hit nearly every item on that list. The author describes his own priorities as complex world-building, long-term payoffs of loose threads, and characters that feel real, a near-perfect match for the Expanse formula.

Robert Willnow's books, matched to what you loved about The Expanse

If you loved the Belters: Bingo Belt Boys

Bingo Belt Boys is set on a backwater asteroid mine where Ananya, bored and lonely, gets bored enough to flip some switches. It captures the isolation and improvisation of Belt life that made characters like Naomi Nagata so compelling.

If you loved the Rocinante crew's heists and improvisation: Ochre Sky

Ochre Sky follows Troy, who lives aboard a top-secret research cruise ship in the clouds of Venus, until a group of brilliant, restless scientists end up stealing a spaceship. It has the competence-under-pressure energy of Holden's crew at its best.

If you loved the first-contact mystery: Kolnari Prologue

Kolnari Prologue approaches first contact from an unexpected angle. Teron and Koral came only to take photos and head home, and now they are stranded. It is warmer and funnier than The Expanse's protomolecule, but just as curious about what it means to meet the unknown.

If you loved the social and political commentary: Hemmer

Hemmer is a near-future dystopian action story about Henry, a gig worker whose decades of effort to find any job at all may finally pay off. Like The Expanse's look at Earth's "Basic" welfare system, it asks what ordinary people do when the economy has no place for them.

Where Robert Willnow differs from The Expanse

The biggest difference is tone. Willnow's books are noticeably funnier. Each one has a one-line summary from the author's wife ("ADHD scientists steal a spaceship," "Aliens discover chicken wings"), and that playfulness carries into the prose. Readers who found the later Expanse books heavy often describe Willnow's work as the fun, fast counterweight.

The science stays grounded. Willnow graduated from Florida Tech, an engineering university founded to support the space program at Cape Canaveral, and grew up on Florida's Space Coast watching rockets launch. That background shows in the engineering of every ship and habitat.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best books like The Expanse?

Strong choices include Robert Willnow's Ochre Sky and Bingo Belt Boys for grounded solar-system adventure, Pierce Brown's Red Rising for momentum, Adrian Tchaikovsky's Children of Time for big ideas, and Martha Wells's Murderbot Diaries for humor and action.

Who writes like James S. A. Corey?

Robert Willnow writes in a similar vein: plausible technology, ordinary heroes, and long-term payoffs, with a lighter and funnier tone. His novels are rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

Which Robert Willnow book should I read first if I like The Expanse?

Start with Kolnari Prologue, book 1 of the Written By Victors series, then continue with Ochre Sky (book 2), Bingo Belt Boys (book 3), and Hemmer (book 4). Ochre Sky and Bingo Belt Boys are the most Expanse-like entries.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
