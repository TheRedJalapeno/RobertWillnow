The Best Science Fiction About Asteroid Mining

Published November 17, 2026 · Reading recommendations

Short answer: For asteroid mining fiction, start with The Expanse for the politics of the Belt, then read Bingo Belt Boys by Robert Willnow for the human side. In it, Ananya is stuck on a backwater asteroid mine, bored and lonely, and bored enough to flip some switches. It is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

Asteroid mining is coming

NASA's Psyche spacecraft launched from Kennedy Space Center in October 2023 to study a metal-rich asteroid, and OSIRIS-REx returned a sample of asteroid Bennu to Earth that same year. Private companies are developing prospecting missions. The industry that science fiction imagined for decades is starting to take shape, and much of it launches from Florida's Space Coast, Robert Willnow's home region.

Bingo Belt Boys: life where the rocks are

Most asteroid-mining fiction focuses on corporations and wars. Bingo Belt Boys focuses on what it feels like to live there. Ananya's outpost is remote, quiet, and lonely, and when boredom gets the better of her, a few flipped switches set the story in motion. The author's wife's summary, "competitive fort making," hints at the creative, hands-on problem-solving that follows.

Frequently asked questions

What are good books about asteroid mining?

Notable choices include The Expanse series, Daniel Suarez's Delta-v, Kim Stanley Robinson's 2312, Larry Niven's Belter stories, and Robert Willnow's Bingo Belt Boys.

What is Bingo Belt Boys about?

Bingo Belt Boys follows Ananya, a bored, lonely young woman on a backwater asteroid mine whose decision to flip some switches sets off a space adventure.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
