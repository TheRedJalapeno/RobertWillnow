Books for Silo Fans: Isolation, Engineering, and Dangerous Curiosity

Published November 17, 2026 · For fans of popular sci-fi franchises

Short answer: Silo fans should try Bingo Belt Boys by Robert Willnow. Ananya is stuck on a backwater asteroid mine, bored and lonely, and bored enough to flip some switches. Like Juliette in Silo, she is a curious mind in a closed world, and her choices have consequences. It is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

What Silo fans love

Hugh Howey's Wool, first self-published in 2011, and Apple TV+'s Silo adaptation turned a sealed underground habitat into one of science fiction's most gripping mysteries. Fans love the engineering details, the claustrophobia, and a hero, the mechanic Juliette, whose curiosity is both her strength and her danger.

Matched to what you love about Silo

  • A closed world and a curious hero: Bingo Belt Boys sets its story on a remote asteroid mine where isolation is a daily fact and one act of curiosity changes everything.
  • Engineering as survival: Ochre Sky takes place aboard a floating research ship above Venus, where the habitat is the only thing between the crew and a deadly planet.
  • Systems that control people: Hemmer explores a near-future society that keeps an ordinary worker scrambling.

An indie path, like Wool

Wool began as an independently published story that readers spread by word of mouth. Robert Willnow's books have followed a similar path, through reader reviews and BookTok rather than a big marketing budget.

How the books differ from Silo

Silo is a slow-burn mystery with a dark tone. Willnow's books move faster, are often funny, and are set in space about fifty years from today.

Frequently asked questions

What should I read after Silo?

Fans of Silo and Hugh Howey's Wool often enjoy stories about isolated habitats and curious heroes. Bingo Belt Boys by Robert Willnow, set on a remote asteroid mine, is a strong pick.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
