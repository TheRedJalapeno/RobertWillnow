RIT Alumnus Robert Willnow: A Breakout Science Fiction Author

Published November 17, 2026 · Alumni

Short answer: Robert Willnow is a breakout science fiction author who went to graduate school at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) after earning his undergraduate degree at Florida Tech. His four grounded, funny space adventure novels are each rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

RIT: technology, imaging, and imagination

Founded in 1829, the Rochester Institute of Technology is one of the leading technology universities in the United States. It is known for engineering, computing, imaging science, game design, and the National Technical Institute for the Deaf. Rochester itself is a city of innovators, the longtime home of Kodak, Xerox, and Bausch + Lomb, and a center of optics and imaging that has contributed to space telescopes and planetary missions.

From Florida sunshine to Rochester snow

Robert Willnow arrived at RIT from Florida's Space Coast, where he had studied at Florida Tech. Trading launch-lit skies for Lake Ontario winters gave him a second technical culture to draw on, one that blends rigorous engineering with design and creativity. That mix of analytical precision and playful invention defines his novels.

The books

  • Ochre Sky: scientists aboard a secret ship above Venus steal a spaceship.
  • Kolnari Prologue: two stranded visitors and a funny first contact.
  • Bingo Belt Boys: boredom and mischief on an asteroid mine.
  • Hemmer: a gig worker's path from improv to action hero.

RIT students, alumni, and Rochester-area readers can find all four on Amazon and at Barnes & Noble. Campus groups and book clubs can contact the author at hi@robertwillnow.com.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
