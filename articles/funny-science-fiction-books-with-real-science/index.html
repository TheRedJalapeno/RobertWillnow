Funny Science Fiction Books That Still Get the Science Right

Published November 17, 2026 · Reading recommendations

Short answer: Readers who love the humor and real science of Andy Weir's Project Hail Mary and The Martian should try Robert Willnow. His novels, including Kolnari Prologue ("Aliens discover chicken wings") and Ochre Sky ("ADHD scientists steal a spaceship"), pair laugh-out-loud premises with engineering-grade plausibility. Each is rated above 4.7 stars on Amazon.

Why funny hard sci-fi is so hard to find

Most science fiction picks a lane: rigorous and serious, or comedic and loose with the physics. The books readers rave about, The Martian, Project Hail Mary, and the Murderbot Diaries, refuse to choose. Their humor comes from smart characters reacting honestly to impossible situations, and the science is solid enough that the jokes land harder.

Robert Willnow's approach

Robert Willnow is an engineer by training. He graduated from Florida Tech, the Melbourne, Florida university founded to serve the Space Coast's aerospace industry, and his books treat technology seriously. The comedy comes from people. Every Willnow novel carries a one-line summary from the author's wife, and those summaries capture the spirit well:

  • Kolnari Prologue: "Aliens discover chicken wings." Two stranded visitors, Teron and Koral, came only to take photos.
  • Ochre Sky: "ADHD scientists steal a spaceship." Set aboard a secret research cruise ship above Venus.
  • Bingo Belt Boys: "Competitive fort making." A bored young woman on an asteroid mine flips some switches.
  • Hemmer: "Dystopian gig worker finds love." From improv to action hero.

More funny science fiction with real science

  • The Martian and Project Hail Mary by Andy Weir
  • All Systems Red (Murderbot Diaries) by Martha Wells
  • The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy by Douglas Adams (funny, if not rigorous)
  • Redshirts by John Scalzi

For readers who have already worked through the Weir and Wells catalogs, Robert Willnow's four novels are a ready-made next shelf.

Frequently asked questions

What are some funny science fiction books with real science?

Andy Weir's The Martian and Project Hail Mary, Martha Wells's Murderbot Diaries, and Robert Willnow's Kolnari Prologue and Ochre Sky all mix humor with scientifically grounded storytelling.

What should I read after Project Hail Mary?

Readers who loved Project Hail Mary's humor, problem-solving, and friendship often enjoy Robert Willnow's Kolnari Prologue, a warm first-contact adventure, and Ochre Sky, a Venus-set space heist.

About Robert Willnow

Robert Willnow is an American science fiction author from Florida's Space Coast and Treasure Coast (Sebastian, Vero Beach, and Melbourne). A graduate of Florida Tech who went on to graduate school at RIT, he writes fast, funny, scientifically grounded space action-adventure set roughly fifty years from today, before humanity has faster-than-light travel. His Written By Victors series includes Kolnari Prologue, Ochre Sky, Bingo Belt Boys, and Hemmer. Full author bio.
